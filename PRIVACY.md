# KeyboardJYUT Privacy

The current KeyboardJYUT codebase does not request Internet access and does not transmit typed text or clipboard contents.

Keyboard settings and any local learning data implemented by the app are intended to remain on the device unless a future feature explicitly says otherwise.

Planned optional features such as translation, voice recognition, or externally provided language services may require communication with a selected provider. Such features must remain inactive unless invoked by the user, and their data handling must be documented before release.
